Departments

Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)

The Computer Science and Engineering department focuses on the design, development, and maintenance of computer systems and software. Students learn programming languages, data structures, algorithms, database management, operating systems, artificial intelligence, machine learning, cybersecurity, cloud computing, and web technologies. Graduates can pursue careers as software developers, data scientists, AI engineers, cybersecurity analysts, and system architects.

Information Technology (IT)

The Information Technology department emphasizes the practical application of computing technologies in businesses and organizations. Students study web development, mobile application development, networking, cloud computing, database administration, system administration, DevOps, and information security. IT graduates work as web developers, network engineers, cloud engineers, database administrators, and IT consultants.

Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE)

The Electronics and Communication Engineering department deals with the design and development of electronic devices and communication systems. Students learn analog and digital electronics, microprocessors, embedded systems, wireless communication, satellite communication, VLSI design, signal processing, and the Internet of Things (IoT). Career opportunities include embedded systems engineer, communication engineer, VLSI designer, IoT developer, and electronics design engineer.

Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE)

The Electrical and Electronics Engineering department focuses on electrical power generation, transmission, distribution, and automation technologies. Students study electrical machines, power electronics, renewable energy systems, control systems, industrial automation, PLCs, robotics, and smart grid technologies. Graduates can work as power system engineers, electrical design engineers, automation engineers, renewable energy specialists, and maintenance engineers.

Mechanical Engineering

The Mechanical Engineering department is one of the oldest engineering disciplines, focusing on the design, analysis, manufacturing, and maintenance of mechanical systems. Students learn engineering mechanics, thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, machine design, CAD/CAM, robotics, automobile engineering, manufacturing processes, and industrial engineering. Career opportunities include mechanical design engineer, production engineer, automotive engineer, robotics engineer, and quality control engineer.
