SILAMBARASAN

Indian actor and playback singer

Early life

Silambarasan TR was born on 3 February 1983 in Madras, Tamil Nadu, as the eldest child of T. Rajendar, a Tamilian, and Usha Rajendar, a Telugu.[5] He has a younger brother Kuralarasan, and a sister Ilakiya. From an early age, he has been a devout Hindu and an ardent follower of Lord Shiva.Silambarasan TR was educated at Don Bosco Matriculation Higher Secondary School, Chennai. He then studied at the Loyola College, Chennai.[6] Silambarasan TR starred in many of his father's films as a child until 2002, when he acted in his father's Kadhal Azhivathillai, his first main role as a hero.[7]

Film career

He began his acting career as a child artist in his father T. Rajendar's films. He made his debut as a lead actor with Kadhal Azhivathillai (2002).He gained popularity through successful films like Kovil (2004), Kuthu (2004), and Manmadhan (2004), with Manmadhan becoming a major turning point in his career. He later starred in notable films such as Thotti Jaya, Saravana, and Vallavan, where he also worked as a writer and director.

In 2010, Vinnaithaandi Varuvaayaa became one of his most acclaimed films and is considered a cult classic. Other successful films include Vaanam (2011), Achcham Yenbadhu Madamaiyada (2016), Chekka Chivantha Vaanam (2018), Eeswaran (2021), Maanaadu (2021), and Pathu Thala (2023).

After a brief break during the pandemic, STR transformed his lifestyle by losing over 30 kg, becoming a vegetarian, and learning Bharatanatyam. He also hosted Bigg Boss Ultimate (2022). He is currently working on STR50, produced under his own banner, and Thug Life, directed by Mani Ratnam alongside Kamal Haasan.
