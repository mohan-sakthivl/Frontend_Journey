Benefits of Joining Porter as a Driver Partner

Becoming a Porter Driver Partner offers the opportunity to work with one of India's leading on-demand logistics platforms while enjoying the flexibility to manage your work schedule. With a technology-driven platform, transparent operations, and dedicated support, Porter enables Driver Partners to provide reliable delivery services while building a stable source of income.

How to Join Porter

Joining Porter as a Driver Partner is simple and convenient.

Step 1 – Register Online

Fill out the registration form with your personal, contact, and vehicle details.

Step 2 – Upload Documents

Submit the required documents for identity and vehicle verification.

Step 3 – Verification

Our team will verify your information and ensure all requirements are met.

Step 4 – Account Approval

Once your application is approved, your Driver Partner account will be activated.

Step 5 – Start Delivering

Log in to the Porter Partner App, accept delivery requests, and begin your journey.

Documents Required

To complete your registration, keep the following documents ready:

  • Valid Driving License
  • Vehicle Registration Certificate (RC)
  • Vehicle Insurance Certificate
  • Pollution Under Control (PUC) Certificate
  • Aadhaar Card or any Government-issued ID
  • PAN Card (if required)
  • Active Bank Account Details

DO's

  • Follow all traffic rules and always wear a helmet or seat belt.
  • Reach pickup and delivery locations on time.
  • Handle customer goods with care and deliver them safely.
  • Communicate politely and professionally with customers
  • Keep your vehicle and documents (DL, RC, Insurance, PUC) up to date.

Don'ts

  • Do not overspeed or drive under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
  • Do not cancel orders without a valid reason.
  • Do not use your mobile phone while driving.
  • Do not overload your vehicle beyond its permitted capacity.
  • Do not misbehave with customers or transport prohibited items.

How Much Can You Earn?

Your earnings as a Porter Driver Partner depend on several factors, including your city, vehicle type, working hours, number of completed trips, travel distance, and customer demand. By staying active during peak hours and maintaining consistent performance, Driver Partners can maximize their earning potential through regular trips and incentive programs. Porter also provides complete transparency by allowing partners to view estimated earnings before accepting a delivery and track daily and weekly payouts directly in the Partner App

Platform Charges

Porter does not publicly disclose a fixed commission rate that applies to all Driver Partners. Platform charges may vary depending on factors such as the city, service category, and applicable programs. Before accepting a trip, partners can view the estimated earnings in the Porter Partner App, ensuring transparency in every delivery.
