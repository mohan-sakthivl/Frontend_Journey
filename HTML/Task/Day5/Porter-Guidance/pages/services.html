KEY FEATURES WE OFFER !!!

1.Unified Trip Details - Check all your goods transportation trip information in the city.

Manage all your transportation activities from a single, centralized dashboard. The Unified Trip Details feature provides complete visibility into every trip, including pickup and drop locations, route information, vehicle details, trip status, estimated distance, and delivery history. Whether you're tracking an ongoing delivery or reviewing completed trips, this feature helps you stay organized, improve operational efficiency, and make informed decisions with accurate trip data always at your fingertips.

2.Payments through Prepaid Wallet - No cash reimbursement hassles, as all trips are prepaid.

Experience a secure and hassle-free payment system with Porter's prepaid wallet. All transportation charges are processed in advance, eliminating the need to handle cash or wait for reimbursement. The wallet provides quick, transparent, and reliable transactions while allowing you to monitor your balance and payment history. This cashless approach simplifies financial management and lets Driver Partners focus entirely on delivering exceptional service.

3.Complete Clarity and Control - Monitor wallet usage with full visibility.

Stay informed with complete transparency over your wallet and trip activities. Access detailed transaction records, monitor wallet balances, review payment history, and track expenses from one convenient location. With clear financial insights and organized records, Driver Partners can confidently manage their earnings, maintain accurate payment information, and make better financial decisions throughout their delivery journey.

4.Multi-User Access - Add, remove, activate, or deactivate users to maintain unified logistics operations.

Efficiently manage logistics operations by providing secure access to multiple authorized users. Administrators can add new users, update permissions, activate or deactivate accounts, and control user access whenever required. This feature supports better collaboration among teams while maintaining security, accountability, and centralized control over day-to-day logistics operations, making it ideal for businesses managing multiple users under a single account.
