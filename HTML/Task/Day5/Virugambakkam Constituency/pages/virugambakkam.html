About Virugambakkam

Virugambakkam is a busy residential and commercial neighbourhood in western Chennai, part of the Virugambakkam constituency. It offers residents easy access to reputed schools and colleges, multi-speciality hospitals, shopping malls, cinemas, and a wide variety of restaurants and clothing stores — all within the neighbourhood or a short drive away in Vadapalani, KK Nagar, and Koyambedu.
