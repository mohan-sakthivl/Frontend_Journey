Infosys

Infosys is a leading Indian multinational information technology (IT) services and consulting company founded in 1981 and headquartered in Bengaluru, Karnataka. It is one of the largest IT companies in India, serving clients in more than 50 countries across industries such as banking, healthcare, retail, manufacturing, telecommunications, and finance. The company focuses on helping businesses accelerate their digital transformation by delivering innovative, reliable, and customer-centric technology solutions.

Infosys offers a wide range of technology and business services, including application development, full stack development, cloud computing, artificial intelligence (AI), data analytics, cybersecurity, DevOps, digital transformation, enterprise application services, and IT consulting. The company also provides software testing, automation, infrastructure management, business process management, and maintenance services. By leveraging modern technologies, Infosys helps organizations improve efficiency, reduce operational costs, and drive business growth.

Infosys has development centers and offices across several major cities in India, where it regularly hires full stack developers, software engineers, and other IT professionals. Some of its major hiring locations include Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Pune, Mysuru, Noida, Gurugram, Mumbai, and Coimbatore. Fresh graduates and experienced professionals are recruited through campus placements, off-campus drives, and lateral hiring to work on projects involving web development, cloud technologies, AI, enterprise applications, and digital transformation.
