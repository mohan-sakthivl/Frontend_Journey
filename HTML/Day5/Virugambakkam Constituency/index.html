About Virugambakkam

Virugambakkam is a well-connected residential and commercial neighbourhood in western Chennai, forming part of the Virugambakkam constituency. It is home to a mix of established schools, colleges, hospitals, and shopping destinations, along with easy access to nearby areas like Vadapalani, KK Nagar, and Koyambedu. With its blend of residential comfort and everyday conveniences, Virugambakkam continues to be a preferred neighbourhood for families and students alike.
