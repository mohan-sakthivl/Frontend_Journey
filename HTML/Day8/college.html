ABOUT SSIET

SREE SASTHA INSTITIONS is one of the leading institutions committed to providing quality education in engineering, technology, and management. The college offers modern infrastructure, experienced faculty, excellent placement opportunities, and industry-oriented learning to help students build successful careers.

Departments

Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)

CSE focuses on computer programming, software development, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, cloud computing, and data science. Students learn programming languages such as C, C++, Java, Python, database management, web development, operating systems, networking, and software engineering.

Career Scope: Software Developer, Full Stack Developer, AI Engineer, Data Scientist, Cloud Engineer, Cybersecurity Analyst, DevOps Engineer, and System Administrator.

Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE)

ECE deals with electronic circuits, communication systems, embedded systems, IoT, VLSI design, signal processing, wireless communication, and microprocessors.

Career Scope: Embedded Systems Engineer, Electronics Design Engineer, Network Engineer, IoT Developer, Communication Engineer, and VLSI Engineer.

Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE)

EEE focuses on electrical machines, power generation, transmission systems, renewable energy, industrial automation, PLC programming, and power electronics.

Career Scope: Electrical Engineer, Power Plant Engineer, Automation Engineer, Maintenance Engineer, and Renewable Energy Specialist.

Civil Engineering

Civil Engineering teaches structural analysis, surveying, construction management, environmental engineering, transportation engineering, and concrete technology.

Career Scope: Site Engineer, Structural Engineer, Project Manager, Construction Consultant, and Government Engineer.

Artificial Intelligence and Data Science (AI & DS)

AI & DS focuses on machine learning, deep learning, data analytics, Python programming, statistics, computer vision, natural language processing, and big data technologies.

Career Scope: AI Engineer, Machine Learning Engineer, Data Scientist, Data Analyst, NLP Engineer, and Business Intelligence Developer.

Admission Process

  1. Application Registration
    Students can apply online through the college admission portal or submit an offline application form at the admission office.
  2. Document Submission
    Applicants must submit their 10th and 12th mark sheets, transfer certificate, community certificate (if applicable), passport-size photographs, Aadhaar card, and other required documents.
  3. Eligibility Verification
    The admission committee verifies the student's academic qualifications and eligibility based on university guidelines.
  4. Counselling / Seat Allocation
    Students participate in counselling based on entrance examination scores or higher secondary marks. Seats are allotted according to merit and category reservation.
  5. Fee Payment
    Selected candidates must pay the admission fee before the specified deadline.
  6. Admission Confirmation
    After successful fee payment and document verification, students receive their admission confirmation letter and student ID.
