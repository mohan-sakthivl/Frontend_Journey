NEET Protest Intensifies Across India - July 22, 2026

Protests over alleged irregularities in the NEET examination continued across several parts of India today. Students, activists, and opposition leaders gathered in multiple cities demanding greater transparency in the examination process and accountability for the reported paper leak.

In New Delhi, demonstrations outside Parliament and other public locations called for reforms in the examination system. The issue also disrupted proceedings during the ongoing Monsoon Session, with opposition parties demanding a discussion and the resignation of Union Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan. The government has stated that it is prepared to discuss the matter in Parliament and has promised reforms to strengthen the examination system.

Similar protests were reported in Chennai, Trichy, Aizawl, and other cities, where students expressed concerns over the fairness and credibility of the medical entrance examination. In Mizoram, hundreds of students marched in Aizawl demanding action over the alleged paper leak.

Students say they want a transparent investigation, stronger safeguards against paper leaks, and measures to ensure that future examinations are conducted fairly. Authorities have appealed for calm while investigations and discussions continue.
